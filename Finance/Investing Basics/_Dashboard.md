## 🧭 Suggested Path
> [!example] If you only do one thing at a time
> Stage 1 → Stage 2 → Stage 5 (start a SIP in an index fund) → Stage 3 → Stage 4 → Stage 9 → Stage 6 → Stage 7 → Stage 8.
> Rationale: secure your finances, understand compounding, start investing simply via index funds *early*, then deepen knowledge. Save trading and charts (6–7) and especially derivatives/F&O (8) for last — they're the riskiest and least necessary for building wealth. Learn F&O to understand it, not to gamble with it.
